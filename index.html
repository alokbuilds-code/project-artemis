INVESTMENT DOSSIER
01 / GENESIS & FOUNDING CAPITAL

The Software Artists

Before scaling into an annualized multi-billion dollar platform, Electronic Arts was conceived on a singular premise: software engineers and designers were artists who commanded equity, recognition, and cultural authority.

FIG. 1.0 — SILICON VALLEY HERITAGE: 8-BIT MICROCOMPUTING ERA TO REDWOOD SHORES HQ (1982–1998)
1982

“We’re not the artists. They are.”

— Steve Hayes, Pajaro Dunes Retreat, November 1982

Trip Hawkins resigned as Director of Product Marketing at Apple following its landmark IPO. Backed with an initial $200,000 personal investment and incubation space from Don Valentine at Sequoia Capital, Hawkins incorporated Amazin’ Software on May 27, 1982. At an offsite retreat at Pajaro Dunes, when executives debated naming the firm “Electronic Artists” in homage to United Artists, Steve Hayes argued that the creators alone held that distinction. Sentiment shifted instantly: Electronic Arts was born.

Album Packaging

Packaged like vinyl records (M.U.L.E., Pinball Construction Set) with photo credits and the creed: “We see farther.”

Amiga & Deluxe Paint

In 1985, EA authored Deluxe Paint. Jerry Morrison authored the Interchange File Format (IFF), standardizing PC computing media.

1987 • Skate or Die!

EA launched its first internally developed title, breaking from pure third-party distribution toward captive studio production.

1990 NASDAQ IPO

Went public under ticker ERTS on March 26, 1990 at a split-adjusted $0.52/share (updated to EA in 2011).

INFRASTRUCTURE & SCALE

The Digital Distribution Engine

Passing $1B in digital bookings in 2011, establishing the Frostbite unified engine, and capturing multi-decade exclusive sports moats.

04 / TRANSACTION MILESTONES

The Deal Timeline

Chronology of the definitive merger agreement, regulatory review phases, shareholder approval, and consummation.

FIG. 4.0 — DUE DILIGENCE & ANTITRUST REVIEW: SEC FORM 10-K AND EUROPEAN COMMISSION CLEARANCE
REGULATORY & LEGAL CLEARANCE

Unconditional Global Approval

Spanning from September 2025 to August 2026, the transaction required international competition reviews. The European Commission approved the buyout in July 2026 without requiring divestments, paving the way for the historic August 4 delisting from public markets.

29 SEP 2025 MILESTONE 01 // ANNOUNCEMENT

Definitive Merger Agreement Announced

Electronic Arts enters into a definitive agreement to be acquired by a consortium comprising Saudi Arabia’s PIF, Silver Lake, and Affinity Partners for $210.00 per share in cash, a 64% premium over closing price. $20 billion in debt financing is committed by JPMorgan Chase.

DEC 2025 MILESTONE 02 // GOVERNANCE

Shareholder Approval Ratified

An overwhelming majority of EA public stockholders vote to approve the take-private transaction, endorsing the $210 cash payout over ongoing public market volatility.

JUL 2026 MILESTONE 03 // ANTITRUST

European Commission Clearance

The European Commission grants unconditional antitrust clearance, concluding the transaction raises no competition concerns due to limited market overlap among the acquiring investment entities.

04 AUG 2026 MILESTONE 04 // CONSUMMATION

Acquisition Formally Completed & Stock Delisted

The $55 billion buyout closes. Electronic Arts common stock is delisted from the NASDAQ and removed from the S&P 500 and NASDAQ-100 indices, concluding 36 years of continuous public market trading.

07 / HEADCOUNT & RATIONALIZATION

The Cost of Scale

A factual accounting of corporate contractions and rationalization across two decades of technology and market volatility.

2006
5% Global Headcount Cut

Larry Probst executes initial enterprise reductions prior to the launch of the PlayStation 3 generation to control soaring HD production costs.

2008–09
2,600 Roles Cut • 12 Studios Closed

Great Recession losses trigger an 11% layoff (1,100 roles) followed by a 17% cut (1,500 roles) across Tiburon, Black Box, and Mythic, culminating in Pandemic Studios’ shutdown.

2013
10% Workforce Rationalization

John Riccitiello steps down following revenue misses. Interim leadership cuts 900 positions to centralize marketing and bring Origin operations under consolidated command.

2023
775 Employees • 6% Workforce

Cancellation of Apex Legends Mobile and Battlefield Mobile leads to Industrial Toys' closure. 200 QA testers in Baton Rouge dismissed over Zoom; 50 BioWare veterans cut.

2024
670 Roles Cut • Star Wars FPS Canceled

Andrew Wilson announces an institutional shift away from third-party licensed IP toward owned franchises, terminating Respawn’s Mandalorian first-person shooter.

2025
< 100 BioWare Headcount Remaining

Underperformance of EA Sports FC 25 and Dragon Age: The Veilguard prompts earnings warnings. BioWare is cut from 200+ to under 100 staff, and Cliffhanger Games is dissolved.

AUGUST 04, 2026 // CORPORATE INVERSION

From Public to Private Entity

Delisted from NASDAQ, S&P 500, and NASDAQ-100 after 36 years. The public market era closes.
